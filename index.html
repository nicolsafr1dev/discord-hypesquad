About This Tool & Safety

Since Discord recently removed the HypeSquad quiz options directly from the settings interface, this tool serves as a simple way to change or clear your HypeSquad badge on your profile.

Is this safe? Yes! This website runs 100% locally in your web browser. No server is hosting this project other than GitHub Pages serving static files. Your token is transmitted directly to Discord's official endpoints using SSL encryption. Your credentials are never stored, sent to third-parties, or logged.

  1. Open Discord in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.) and log in.
  2. Press F12 or Ctrl + Shift + I to open the Developer Tools.
  3. Click on the Network tab.
  4. Reload the page (press F5) to capture the network requests.
  5. In the filter box at the top, type /api.
  6. Click on any request that shows up (like science, users, or profile).
  7. Go to the Headers sub-tab and scroll down to the Request Headers section.
  8. Copy the entire string value of the header named authorization: (this is your token).
IMPORTANT WARNING: Never share your token with anyone. Having your token allows full control over your Discord account. This script communicates directly with the official Discord API.
